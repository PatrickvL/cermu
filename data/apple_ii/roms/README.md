# Apple II Family ROM Files

ROMs required by the Apple II, Apple IIe, and Apple IIc emulation.

## Required ROMs

### Apple II (original)

| Filename | Size | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| `apple2.rom` | 16 KB | Apple II ROM — Applesoft BASIC + Monitor ($C000–$FFFF) |

### Apple IIe (enhanced)

| Filename | Size | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| `apple2e.rom` | 16 KB | Apple IIe ROM — Enhanced Applesoft + Monitor ($C000–$FFFF) |

### Apple IIc

| Filename | Size | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| `apple2c.rom` | 32 KB | Apple IIc ROM — ROM 0 + ROM 1 (bank-switched at $C000–$FFFF) |

## Alternative Filenames Accepted

- **Apple II:** `apple2.rom`, `APPLE2.ROM`, `apple2o.rom`
- **Apple IIe:** `apple2e.rom`, `APPLE2E.ROM`, `apple2e_enhanced.rom`
- **Apple IIc:** `apple2c.rom`, `APPLE2C.ROM`, `apple2c_v4.rom`

## Where to Obtain

These ROMs are copyrighted by Apple Inc. and are **not** included in this
repository. To obtain them legally:

- **Dump from original hardware** — if you own an Apple II/IIe/IIc
- **MAME ROM set** — `apple2.zip`, `apple2e.zip`, `apple2c.zip`
- **Apple II community archives** — preservation sites with ROM information
  - https://www.apple2.org/
  - https://apple2online.com/

## Hardware Notes

The Apple II family (1977–1988) was designed by Steve Wozniak:

- **Apple II (1977):** MOS 6502 @ 1.023 MHz, up to 48KB RAM
- **Apple IIe (1983):** 65C02 @ 1.023 MHz, 128KB (64KB + 64KB aux), 80-column
- **Apple IIc (1984):** 65C02 @ 1.023 MHz, 128KB, built-in 5.25" floppy

All models use software-generated video via "soft switches" at $C000–$C0FF.
No dedicated video chip — the display is generated by scanning RAM at
specific addresses. Display modes include text (40/80 columns), lo-res
(40×48, 16 colors), hi-res (280×192), and double hi-res (560×192, IIe/IIc).

Audio is a 1-bit speaker toggle at address $C030.
